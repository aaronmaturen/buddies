Fussbuddy

A little pet buddy that lives on your Mac desktop.

Looking after it

  • Right-click your buddy, or use the menu bar icon, to feed it, play, give a gift, clean up, give medicine, turn the lights off or scold it.
  • It calls with a "!" when it needs something. Leaving it waiting counts against you, and how you look after it decides what it grows into.
  • Play is a game of "which hand?": click the fist you think holds the gift. Find it 3 times out of 5 for a happy heart.
  • Drag it anywhere on your screen. Pick its size and turn the sound or Open at Login on or off from the menu bar.
