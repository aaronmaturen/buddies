Eyes lead, the head follows

The eyes reach a new target on a stiff spring. The head follows on a softer one a beat later, and the gap between them is what reads as attention.

Features on an invisible sphere

Each eye and brow sits on the surface of a sphere and is drawn flat against it. Turning the sphere slides and foreshortens them, so you see a head that isn't drawn.

Personality is motion

A buddy's config sets its looks and also its spring stiffness, blink rate, how often it glances around and how much it cares about your cursor.
