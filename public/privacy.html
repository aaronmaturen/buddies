Privacy

Fussbuddy for Mac, and this site. Updated November 4, 2026.

Fussbuddy collects nothing

Fussbuddy has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet. Your pet and your settings (its size, where it sits, sound) are saved only on your Mac, inside the app's own folder. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Deleting the app deletes your pet with it.

This site

Build a Buddy runs in your browser. It has no accounts or analytics, and remembers only which view you last used, in your browser's own storage. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees the request, as it does on any site that uses them. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which keeps standard server logs.
